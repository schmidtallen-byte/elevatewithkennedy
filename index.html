How to Prepare

Prep for your
best glow.

Proper prep is the secret to a streak-free, long-lasting tan. Follow these steps for flawless results.

01
24–48 Hours Before
  • Exfoliate your entire body with an oil-free scrub
  • Shave or wax — do not do this day-of
  • Avoid self-tanners or spray tans for at least 1 week
02
Day of Appointment
  • Arrive with clean, completely dry skin
  • No lotions, oils, deodorant, or perfume
  • Wear loose, dark clothing — this becomes your tan line
  • Remove nail polish if you want hands fully covered
03
After Your Tan
  • First rinse: cool water only, no soap
  • Pat dry — never rub
  • Avoid sweating, swimming, or tight clothing for 12 hrs
  • Moisturize daily to extend your glow

FAQ

Common questions.

How long will my tan last?
On average, 7–10 days. Factors like skin type, activities, and aftercare can affect longevity. Daily moisturizing is the single biggest thing you can do to extend your glow.
Will I turn orange?
Not with Sjolie solutions. Kennedy uses premium, naturally derived DHA that reacts with your skin's specific chemistry. Every tan is custom-blended to your undertones — warm, cool, or neutral — for a natural bronze result.
What's the difference between Classic and Rapid?
The Classic Glow uses a standard formula that develops over 12–24 hours before your first rinse. The Rapid Glow uses an 18% DHA formula that allows you to rinse in just 3–5 hours while still developing fully for up to 24 hours after. Great for busy schedules.
When should I book before an event?
We recommend booking 2–3 days before a major event like a wedding or gala. For vacations, book 1–2 days before you leave. First-timers: consider a trial run 2–3 weeks before your event so you know exactly what to expect.
What do I wear during my session?
Wear whatever you're comfortable in — bikini, strapless bra and underwear, or nothing at all. Whatever you choose becomes your "tan lines," so plan accordingly. After your session, wear loose, dark, breathable clothing.
Where do you travel for mobile appointments?
Kennedy services Suwanee, Forsyth County, North Fulton, Alpharetta, Johns Creek, Cumming, and surrounding North Atlanta areas. Not sure if your location is covered? Just reach out and ask — we'll do our best to accommodate.
Can I get a tan if I'm pregnant?
We do not provide services during the first trimester. After your first trimester, tanning is available with a completed consent form, which can be signed at your appointment. Consult your OB if you have any concerns.
Do you offer bridal packages?
Yes! We love working with brides. We offer a bridal trial tan (recommended 2–6 weeks before your wedding day) so you can experience the process and dial in your exact shade before the big day. Inquire via email for bridal pricing and packages.
